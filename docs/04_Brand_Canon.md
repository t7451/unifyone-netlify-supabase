## Brand Foundation

### Mission

**Give every gig and 1099 worker the earnings clarity and tax confidence the platforms never built for them — engineered to last, not to chase the next app.**

> UnifyOne also retains a secondary multi-tenant commerce capability for operators who need it; the gig earnings and tax product is the front door.

### Vision

A world where the 76M+ Americans who drive, deliver, and freelance know exactly what they earned, what they owe, and what they keep — without a CPA or a spreadsheet. Where IRS mileage, quarterly estimated taxes, and shift intelligence run on autopilot. Where workers focus on the next gig, not the paperwork.

### Core Values

1. **Structural Integrity** — We build the earnings and tax foundation first, then add surface features second
2. **Transparency** — Every mileage log, earnings entry, and tax estimate is recorded, versioned, and auditable
3. **Autonomy** — Tax Autopilot should handle the routine (IRS mileage, quarterly 1040-ES) without intervention; workers override when they need to
4. **Craftsmanship** — We build for workers who depend on accuracy at tax time, not for trends

## Messaging Framework

### Positioning Statement

**For:** The 76M+ US gig and 1099 workers driving, delivering, and freelancing across DoorDash, Uber, Lyft, Instacart, Amazon Flex, and Grubhub  
**Who:** Need to know what they earn, track IRS mileage, and pay the right quarterly estimated taxes — without a CPA or a spreadsheet  
**UnifyOne:** Is a gig-worker earnings and tax app — GigIQ shift/earnings intelligence, Tax Autopilot, Money Manager, and Kai, an AI sidekick  
**That:** Turns scattered platform payouts into clear earnings, deductible miles, and on-time 1040-ES estimates  
**Unlike:** Generic mileage trackers, spreadsheets, or year-end-only tax software  
**We:** Prioritize earnings clarity and tax confidence over feature velocity

### Key Messages

#### Message 1: Earnings Clarity

> "Gig work scatters your money across a half-dozen apps. UnifyOne's GigIQ pulls earnings and shift data into one clear picture — what you made, where, and when — so you can see your real hourly take, not just the platform's headline number."

#### Message 2: Tax Autopilot

> "The routine tax work should run itself. Tax Autopilot tracks deductible IRS mileage and projects your quarterly estimated taxes (Form 1040-ES) automatically — recorded, auditable, and reversible. You override when you need to; it handles the rest."

#### Message 3: Your Numbers Are Yours

> "Your earnings and mileage records belong to you. UnifyOne keeps a complete, exportable history of every entry and estimate. You can pull your full dataset at any time — at tax time, for a CPA, or to move on without friction."

### Tone of Voice

- **Direct:** No corporate jargon, no marketing fluff
- **Plain-spoken:** Assume a working driver or freelancer, not an accountant — explain taxes and mileage in human terms
- **Confident:** We've built the earnings and tax foundation; we're not guessing
- **Transparent:** We show our work (mileage logs, earnings records, tax estimates you can audit)

## Brand Guidelines

### Website Standards

- **Color Scheme:** Pure black background, gold accents, white text
- **Typography:** Cinzel for headlines, Crimson Pro for body
- **Spacing:** 16px grid, consistent padding/margins
- **Images:** High-quality, cathedral/architecture aesthetic
- **CTAs:** Gold buttons with white text, no rounded corners

### Social Media Standards

- **Profile Image:** Gold cross on black (32×32px)
- **Header Image:** Cathedral architecture with UnifyOne wordmark
- **Post Format:** Cinzel headline, Crimson Pro body, gold accent color
- **Tone:** Direct, technical, transparent
- **Hashtags:** #UnifyOne #CathedralFramework #CommerceInfrastructure

- **Subject Line:** Direct, benefit-driven, no clickbait
- **Header:** Cathedral background with gold accent
- **Body:** Crimson Pro, left-aligned, max 600px width
