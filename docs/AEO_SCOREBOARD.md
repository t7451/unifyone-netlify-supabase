# AEO Scoreboard — UnifyOne Share of Voice in AI Answers

Track UnifyOne's presence in AI answer engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI
Overviews) over time. Re-pull the `allmentions` export monthly and add a row.

## Metric definitions
- **Answers** — total AI responses in the export.
- **Mention rate** — % of answers where UnifyOne is named.
- **Citation rate** — % of answers that cite a UnifyOne-owned domain
  (`1commercesolutions.com`, `1commerce.online`, `unifyone.com`, `unifiedcommerce.app`).
- **Unbranded mentions** — mentions on prompts that did **not** contain our
  brand name. This is the real growth metric; branded mentions are easy.

## Targets
- **Day 30:** ≥1 unbranded mention (target topic: Multi-Tenant Architecture).
- **Day 60:** mention rate ≥8%; ≥1 citation of an owned domain on an unbranded prompt.
- **Day 90:** mention rate ≥12%; unbranded mentions across ≥3 topics.
