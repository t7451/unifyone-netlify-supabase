# Marketing direction — October 27, 2026

Keith selected a practical work-tool direction: dark ink, warm orange, and real
product screens.

- Public pages use charcoal (#111416), orange (#f39450), sans-serif headings,
  compact navigation, and simple separators. The calculator directory on the
  homepage uses a contrasting light surface.
- The homepage shows the actual Work Insights and mobile Work History components,
  captured with synthetic records and labeled as an example workspace. No customer
  results, endorsements, or live bank connections are implied.
- Copy leads with work records, time, pay, costs, imports, and accountant exports.
  The illustrated dashboard, decorative chart, AI prompt panel, repeated feature
  cards, and repeated homepage pricing/closing sections were removed.
- Pricing and signup destinations are unchanged. The paid catalog remains the
  source of prices and entitlements; no popularity badge is rendered.
- Public changes are isolated from authenticated application styling. Product
  preview images are static WebP assets, not account data or embedded live pages.

Validated with desktop/mobile visual review, public navigation and signup-link
browser checks, annual billing selection, loaded preview assets, TypeScript,
scoped lint, public crawl/link tests, SEO gates, and a production build.
