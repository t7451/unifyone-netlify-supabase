# Remaining tax guide review — November 7, 2026

Scope: 51 state/DC, 5 city and 22 remaining platform guides. DoorDash and Uber retain the prior review. Chime copy, placement data, editorial section and renderer are unchanged.

## Method and boundaries

Research used the responsible tax agency, platform or its named processor. Sources and tax-year limitations appear in the articles. A source describing 2025 forms delivered in 2026 is not treated as verified 2026 delivery instructions. Public research does not validate individual account documents, personalized tax liability, every entity structure or every local tax. No filing or payment is performed.

State content separates income from gross-receipts/business taxes and does not assume federal deductions, withholding thresholds or installment schedules govern every jurisdiction. Where a current numerical rate was not established, obsolete approximate rates were removed rather than relabeled current. The following matrix records the reviewed source for each route.

## Material corrections

- Philadelphia BIRT exemption repeal beginning with tax year 2025; separate NPT credit and first-time-filer estimate relief.
- Portland 2026 SHS thresholds and business-license exemption; lower income is not a blanket exemption from business filings.
- Idaho/Utah individual estimated-payment exceptions; Virginia May 1 and California unequal installments.
- WA B&O, NH BPT/BET, OR transit self-employment tax, HI GET, NM/DE gross receipts and DC unincorporated business tax.
- Updated year-specific rates/structure where verified, including GA, NC, IN, KY, LA, IA, OK, UT, SC, MD, ME and WV; NY MCTMT threshold is $150,000 in 2026.
- Favor and GoShare use documented 1099-K reporting rather than assumed NEC; Taskrabbit fees/reimbursements require actual reconciliation.
- Dolly rebrand to Taskrabbit Delivery; do not assume marketplace and Delivery tax forms are identical.
- Marketplace personal gains/losses, business sales and hobby activity distinguished.

## Source limitations

Amazon Flex public FAQ retrieval was blocked; the article links Amazon Tax Central and does not assert a verified 2026 form subtype or delivery date. Roadie Prop 22 guidance does not prove a universal payment/form mapping. Gopuff and Wag public guidance describes the 2025 cycle. Veho/Routable confirms 1099 automation, not a specific current driver form subtype. Dolly transition guidance does not establish tax-document continuity. Instacart delivery instructions may differ by account/year. These limitations are visible in the corresponding articles, not hidden in this log.

Automated link checks are recorded separately during validation: a 403, 429 or timeout does not by itself establish a broken source. Official indexed text was also used where direct retrieval was blocked.
