# The problem with money advice in 2026

Every financial app tells you the same three things: budget harder, invest
earlier, save more. None of it works if you don't know what you actually
earn.

Ask a rideshare driver their hourly rate and you'll get a wrong answer —
not because they're careless, but because no one has ever handed them the
math. Gas, depreciation, platform fees, quarterly estimates, the hour of
deadhead between gigs — it all lives in separate places, and the apps
themselves have zero incentive to show you the net number.

Same story for freelancers. Stripe pays out cleanly, but the client who
took three months to approve scope, then negotiated 20% off at the end,
then paid via ACH with a $15 fee? Your "$8,500 project" might have been
$42 an hour. You had no way to know.

Same story for small businesses. Shopify reports gross sales; Stripe
takes 2.9% + $0.30; returns come out later; Meta Ads spend eats the
rest. The dashboard you look at shows none of these interactions.

## What UnifyOne is

A context engine for your own money. Kai reads the places your income
actually comes from — Uber, DoorDash, Stripe, Square, PayPal, Shopify —
and answers questions grounded in *your numbers*, not a stock dataset.

- "What's my real hourly last week after gas?"
- "Which client pays best, adjusted for scope creep?"
- "If I keep this burn, when does my runway end?"
- "What do I owe in quarterly taxes right now?"

Not advice. Not budgeting rules. Receipts.
