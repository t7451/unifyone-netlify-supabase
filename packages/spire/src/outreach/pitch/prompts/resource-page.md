# Resource-page ask (Spire outreach)

Drafting cold email asking for inclusion on a curated resource/tools page.

Context:

- Their resource page: {{SOURCE_PAGE_URL}}
- Page topic theme: {{PAGE_TOPIC}}
- Our asset: {{ASSET_URL}} — "{{ASSET_TITLE}}"
- Why our asset fits: {{MATCH_RATIONALE}}
- Specific reason this list is missing us: {{GAP_RATIONALE}}

Constraints:

- Subject: 5-9 words, references the resource list directly. Example: "addition to your gig income tools list?".
- Open by referencing the specific resource page (proves we read it).
- One concrete reason ours fits — operator-built, specific data point, or unique angle.
- Acknowledge that the decision is theirs. Don't insist.
- 90-140 words.
- No "great resource! love your site!" — assume they know.
- Sign off: "Thanks, {{FROM_NAME}}".

Output JSON:
{
"subject": "...",
"body": "..."
}
