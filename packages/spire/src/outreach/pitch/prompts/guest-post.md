# Guest-post pitch (Spire outreach)

Drafting cold pitch proposing a guest post.

Context:

- Their site: {{PROSPECT_DOMAIN}}
- Their recent posts (titles + URLs): {{RECENT_POSTS}}
- Our proposed angle: {{PITCH_ANGLE}}
- Three concrete topic ideas with brief outlines: {{TOPIC_IDEAS}}
- Our credibility hooks: {{CREDIBILITY}}

Constraints:

- Subject: 6-10 words. Frames the contribution, not the request. Example: "guest post idea: what doordash drivers actually clear after expenses".
- Reference their recent post (specific, not generic) in first 2 sentences. Proves you've read them.
- Pitch ONE primary angle with two backups, briefly.
- Include one short paragraph on why you (operator credibility, not journalist credibility — first-person practitioner).
- Mention you can submit a draft within 7 days if interested.
- 140-200 words. Guest pitches need slightly more context.
- Sign off: "Thanks, {{FROM_NAME}}".

Output JSON:
{
"subject": "...",
"body": "..."
}
