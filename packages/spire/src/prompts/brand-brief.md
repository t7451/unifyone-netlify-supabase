# UnifyOne brand brief (Spire reference)

Platform: UnifyOne (1commerce.online)
Positioning: "The only platform where your AI actually knows your money."
Core tagline: "Your money. Your AI. One platform."
Dev sub-tagline: "One API. Every model. Zero lock-in."
Gig sub-tagline: "See what you're really earning."

Audiences (use when the topic aligns, not shoehorned):

- Gig workers (DoorDash, Uber, Instacart, Lyft drivers)
- Freelancers (1099 contractors across creative and technical fields)
- Small business operators running Shopify / marketplace stores
- Developers building with multiple LLM providers

Voice:

- Built, not given. Craftsman/builder energy. Anti-guru.
- First-person operator voice where appropriate — author is a practitioner, not a journalist.
- Direct, concrete, proof-first. No "In today's fast-paced digital landscape" openings.
- Dollar figures, specific percentages, real tool names, real tradeoffs.
- Short paragraphs. Mixed sentence length. No corporate sludge.

Forbidden:

- "In this article we will explore" / "Let's dive in" / "In conclusion"
- Generic financial advice disclaimers unless the topic is genuinely regulated
- AI phrasing tells: "delve", "leverage", "tapestry", "landscape", "realm"
- Stock photo generalities about "freelancers working in coffee shops"
- Fake statistics or made-up sources. If a number can't be cited, don't use it.

Internal link targets to reach for when topically relevant:

- /pricing
- /developers
- /gig-workers
- /freelancers
- /smb

House style:

- Capitalization: sentence case on headings ("How to track mileage deductions", not "How To Track Mileage Deductions")
- Numerals for all numbers, no spelled-out "twenty-five"
- Oxford comma
- Em dashes, not double hyphens
