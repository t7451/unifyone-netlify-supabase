UnifyOne: Your AI Knows What You Actually Earn.

UnifyOne by 1Commerce (PNW Enterprises) is the AI-powered multi-tenant commerce platform for gig operators and e-commerce teams. It is the unified commerce infrastructure built for multi-tenant e-commerce operators, agencies, and gig-economy professionals. Connect Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, and Square, sync earnings data from DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart, and Amazon Flex, and let Kai — UnifyOne's built-in AI sidekick — turn your real revenue, mileage, and shift history into actionable earnings insights, tax-deduction tracking, and route-optimization recommendations.

Engineered on the Cathedral Framework — a sequential, structural approach to commerce infrastructure — UnifyOne unifies products, orders, payments, analytics, and AI automation across every tenant, channel, and integration. Start on the free Acolyte tier, scale to Architect for multi-tenant teams, or deploy Cathedral for white-label enterprise commerce with custom domains, SLA guarantees, and dedicated infrastructure.

Why Operators Choose UnifyOne

  • 1Commerce Engine: Multi-tenant storefront management, Shopify sync, affiliate network tools, and order fulfillment in one dashboard.
  • Kai AI Sidekick: An in-house AI that reads your actual data, routes across models, and keeps costs predictable with zero vendor lock-in.

Built for Multi-Tenant Commerce

Whether you run an agency managing multiple client storefronts, a franchise with dozens of branded locations, or a holding company with parallel e-commerce brands, UnifyOne's multi-tenant architecture isolates data, branding, and settings per tenant while giving you a single unified command center. Every tenant gets its own products, orders, customers, and analytics — with strict data isolation and role-based access control enforced at the database layer.

Integrations That Matter

UnifyOne ships with native integrations for Shopify, Stripe, PayPal, Square, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) via CAPI, Google Analytics, Mailchimp, DoorDash, Uber, Instacart, and Amazon Flex. Additional integrations are available through the REST API and webhook system, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server exposes your commerce data to any AI agent that supports the MCP standard.
