Frequently Asked Questions

Does UnifyOne work with DoorDash and Uber Eats?
Yes. UnifyOne connects directly to DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart, Amazon Flex, Lyft, and other gig platforms to pull your real earnings history. It calculates your after-expense net income and tracks IRS deductions automatically.

What is the Cathedral Framework?
The Cathedral Framework is UnifyOne's architectural philosophy: build infrastructure sequentially and structurally, like a cathedral — each layer load-bearing, each feature permanent. It means no throwaway MVPs, no tech debt shortcuts, no features built to be replaced.
Who is Kai?
Kai is UnifyOne's built-in AI sidekick. Unlike chatbots that answer generic questions, Kai reads your actual earnings data, shift history, and commerce metrics to give personalized insights. Kai routes queries across 300+ AI models internally and keeps costs predictable with a single monthly charge.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. UnifyOne uses bank-level TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 at rest. Multi-tenant data isolation is enforced at the database layer — tenant data is never co-mingled. The platform is SOC 2 aligned and never sells your data to third parties.
